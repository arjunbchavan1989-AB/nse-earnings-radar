Methodology & definitions

positive negative delayed positive

Sales YoY / EPS YoY — reported quarter vs the same fiscal quarter a year earlier, consolidated, from screener.in quarterly tables (sales in ₹ crore, EPS in ₹ per share). Sign-flipping EPS (loss→profit etc.) is labelled explicitly instead of showing a growth %.

Beat / Miss — surprise % vs analyst consensus EPS estimate (Yahoo Finance). Coverage is best for large caps; “no est.” means no verified consensus exists for that quarter — nothing is imputed. Yahoo's reported actual EPS can differ from screener.in's consolidated EPS (different reporting basis); the detail view shows both side by side.

Day-1 reaction — close of the first session that fully follows the announcement vs the prior close. After-close and weekend releases roll to the next session.

Drift +5 / +10 / +20 — post-earnings announcement drift (PEAD): close 5/10/20 trading sessions after the reaction session vs the reaction close. Incomplete windows show as pending.

Delayed positive — screening rule, not a predictive signal: day-1 ≤ 0% and a close above the reaction session’s high within the next 5 sessions. The trigger day is shown in the detail view. Events whose 5-session window hasn’t fully elapsed yet show as pending (…).

Prices are dividend/split-adjusted daily closes (Yahoo Finance).
